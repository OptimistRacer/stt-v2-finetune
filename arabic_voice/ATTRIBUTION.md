# Arabic voice -- licences

- Speech model: **Chatterbox multilingual** by Resemble AI (`ResembleAI/chatterbox`), MIT licence.
  Its output carries Resemble AI's imperceptible "Perth" watermark, which marks the audio as AI-generated.
- Voice: a **synthetic** reference clip designed with **VoxCPM2** by OpenBMB (`openbmb/VoxCPM2`,
  Apache 2.0, "free for commercial use") from a written description -- not a real person's voice.
- Both models' terms forbid impersonation, fraud and disinformation; VoxCPM2 asks that AI-generated
  audio be labelled. Neither discloses its training-data sources -- note for legal review, as with
  the Urdu voice.
